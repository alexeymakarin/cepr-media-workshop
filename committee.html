Organization

Local organization alternates with the venue between Rome and Milan. The Rome editions (2022, 2023, 2026) are hosted by EIEF and organized jointly with Tor Vergata University — with Stefano Gagliarducci, Mounu Prem, and Francesco Sobbrio serving as local organizing co-chairs in 2026. The Milan edition (2025) was hosted and organized by Università Bocconi. The full organizing committee for each edition is listed in that year’s program.
