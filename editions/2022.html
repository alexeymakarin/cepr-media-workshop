EIEF, Rome — June 24–25, 2022

Einaudi Institute for Economics and Finance, Via Sallustiana 62 · Eleven papers, two keynotes, and a policy round table · Talks: 45 minutes = 25 (author) + 10 (discussant) + 10 (Q&A)
